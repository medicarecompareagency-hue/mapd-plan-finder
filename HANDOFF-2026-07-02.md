### LIS lesser-of bug (found + fixed, commit 67346c9)
`drugTierCellQ` in `app/plan-search.tsx` was showing flat LIS copay without the federal "lesser-of" rule. For 292 DSNP plans where plan T1=$0, display was incorrectly showing $1.60 (full-benefit LIS copay) instead of min($0, $1.60) = $0. Fix: `const effective = Math.min(rawValue, lisV)`.
